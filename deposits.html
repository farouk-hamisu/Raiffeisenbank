How to deposit Bitcoin

  1. Choose the account to credit and enter the amount you want to deposit, then create a deposit request.
  2. We reserve a unique amount for your request — this is what makes your payment identifiable.
  3. Copy the address or scan the QR code and send exactly that amount from your wallet on the Bitcoin network.
  4. Wait for the network confirmations required (shown in your deposit history).
  5. Once confirmed, your balance and transaction history update automatically.
