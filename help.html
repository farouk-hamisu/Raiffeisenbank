Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send money?

Go to the Send Money page, enter the recipient's details, amount, and confirm with your PIN. You'll receive a verification code to complete the transfer.

How long do transfers take?

Local transfers are instant. International transfers typically take 1-3 business days depending on the destination country.

Is my money safe?

Yes. We use bank-level encryption and security measures to protect your funds and personal information. All accounts are monitored 24/7.

How do I reset my PIN?

Go to Settings > Security > Reset PIN. You'll need to verify your identity with a code sent to your email.
